# Brag Plan: Five Days of Product

## What is this app?
A 7-page companion microsite for a 5-day Digital Product Management course — frameworks, glossary, templates, a prompt library, pricing models, and one page per course day, rebuilt from real MBA lecture material.

## The angle
This isn't a startup demo — it's a syllabus that got a real design system. The joke/hook is treating course content with the same visual confidence as a product launch: crisp OKLCH palette, serif display type, a genuine academic debate as the emotional centerpiece. The video should feel like a "quietly excellent" course trailer, not a SaaS ad.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Display serif type on royal-blue-tinted paper: "Five Days of Product." Then subtitle slams in under it: "Everything a PM actually does. In five days." Confident, editorial, not hype.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Value Quadrant: four terms (Valuable / Usable / Feasible / Viable) arriving one by one as clean bordered cards on the paper background.
- The toolkit nav: Glossary → Templates → Prompt Library → Pricing Models labels ticking past like a real sidebar being scrolled/clicked.
- The debate section: Ben Horowitz's "CEO of the product" quote across from Martin Eriksson's rebuttal — a real intellectual tension the site resolves.

## Outro / punchline
Resolution line lands: "Influence without authority is the job." Beat. Then wordmark + URL.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: hero loads on Overview page.
2. Key action: sidebar nav clicked through toolkit pages (Glossary, Templates, Prompt Library, Pricing) — real in-app navigation, not a diagram.
3. Result: lands on Day 1, then the debate/resolution block as the emotional payoff.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium course microsite
- Interpretation: fewer, longer-held scenes; restrained typography-led motion; no jokes, no chaos — confidence comes from stillness and precise type, not speed.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 19s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `oklch(0.985 0.004 262)` (paper, ≈ #f8f9fb)
- Accent: `oklch(0.37 0.128 262)` (brand royal blue, ≈ #0a3d7a) + `oklch(0.43 0.19 5)` (berry, ≈ #9e0b45)
- Text: `oklch(0.30 0.02 262)` (ink, ≈ #2b2f36)
- Display font: Philosopher (serif)
- Body font: Mulish
- Strongest visual element: the royal-blue/berry OKLCH palette against warm-white paper, paired with the Value Quadrant card grid and the two-sided debate blockquote layout.

## Share copy (draft)
Five days, five frameworks, zero fluff — a product management crash course built from an actual MBA syllabus.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm restrained corporate bed, low swell into the resolution line, fade on outro
- Music: calm, confident instrumental bed — piano/strings-adjacent, no percussion-forward tracks
- Music treatment: starts under the hook at low volume, gentle swell entering the debate scene, soft fade-out under the outro wordmark
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time; target 1 strong cue at the Value Quadrant card-in moment, 1 at the debate resolution line
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft card-arrival tick per quadrant card, one subtle page-turn/click sound on nav ticks — professional restraint, no whooshes
- Audio-coupled moments: quadrant cards arriving one-by-one, nav labels ticking past
- Restraint rule: no chaotic stingers, no whoosh transitions, no beat-flash text

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4s
Paper background. "Five Days of Product" slams in centered in Philosopher serif, royal blue. 0.6s later, subtitle in Mulish fades/slides up beneath: "Everything a PM actually does. In five days."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: calm confidence, establish the bed
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: warm instrumental bed starts quiet
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Value Quadrant — 5s
Four bordered cards arrive one by one left-to-right on the paper background: "Valuable" / "Usable" / "Feasible" / "Viable", each with a one-line description beneath in ink-soft.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 cards arrive one by one, ~0.35s stagger, each settles for readable hold
Audio intent: precise, satisfying arrival
Audio-coupled idea: soft card-arrival tick per card
Music: bed continues, subtle lift
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Toolkit nav — 4s
Simulated sidebar: nav labels tick past in sequence — Glossary, Templates, Prompt Library, Pricing Models — each briefly highlighted in berry as if clicked, then settles.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 nav items highlight in sequence, simulating real clicks through the app
Audio intent: light momentum, feels like real use
Audio-coupled idea: subtle click sound per nav highlight
Music: bed steady
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The debate — 4.5s
Two-sided blockquote layout: Horowitz's "CEO of the product" quote on the left in berry-wash panel, Eriksson's rebuttal on the right in blue-wash panel. Beat, then resolution line displaces both: "Influence without authority is the job."
Sequential/interaction: none (two panels present together, then resolution line replaces them)
Audio intent: brief tension, then the swell resolves on the punchline
Audio-coupled idea: music swell timed to resolution line arrival
Music: gentle swell peaks here
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 1.5s
Wordmark "Five Days of Product" + URL, centered on paper, small and confident.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: settle, fade
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: fades out
Transition mood: soft fade → end
