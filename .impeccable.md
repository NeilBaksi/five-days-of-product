# Five Days of Product — Project Notes

## Design Context

### Users
MBA students, early-career PMs, and curious professionals who want the fundamentals
of Digital Product Management without sitting the course. They read on a laptop or
phone, one day/topic at a time, and expect to skim, land on a framework, and go.
Australian / Melbourne Business School academic framing.

### Brand Personality
Academic, precise, quietly authoritative — a well-set course reader, not a SaaS
landing page. Three words: **scholarly, clear, confident**. Emotional goal: trust
and calm competence. Companion to the sibling site *Prompt Toolkit* (same quality bar).

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial / Swiss-modern, **light mode only**, MBS-inspired academic-corporate.
Cool off-white background (`paper` #f8f9fb), blue-tinted near-black ink (#2b2f36),
deep royal blue primary (`brand` #0a3d7a) with a berry-crimson secondary
(`berry` #9e0b45) used rarely. Big serif numerals mark sections. Anti-references:
neon-on-dark AI landing pages, gradient text, glassmorphism, identical boxed card
grids, dashboards.

### Typography
- Display: **Philosopher** (400/700) — characterful display face for headlines &
  section numerals; warmer, more editorial than a neutral serif. Headings use
  `font-bold` (700, its max weight — no synthetic 900).
- Body: **Mulish** (300–700) — clean rounded-geometric sans.
- Mono: **JetBrains Mono** — kickers, labels, badges, day markers (functional only).

### Design Principles
1. **Accent is rare.** Blue + berry ≤10% of visual weight (60-30-10). Never fill areas.
2. **SVG icons only** (lucide-react). No large rounded icon above every heading.
3. **No AI tells.** No gradient text, no `border-left`/`border-right` accent stripes
   (callouts use full tinted-panel backgrounds instead), no glassmorphism, no pure
   black/white, no identical card grids.
4. **Motion is orchestrated.** One staggered page-load per route; scroll reveals;
   sidebar active-pill `layoutId`; drawer slide. Transform/opacity only; honour
   `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Mobile is first-class.** Sidebar → top hamburger drawer (focus-trapped);
   tables reflow to stacked `<dl>`; tap targets ≥44px; body never h-scrolls.
6. **Content is data.** All copy lives in `src/data/*` — nav, frameworks, day
   content — never hard-coded in JSX. Real lecture content drops into these files.
7. **Placeholder is obvious.** Genuinely-empty slots use `<Placeholder>` (dashed
   border + mono "PLACEHOLDER" label); structural chrome uses realistic dummy prose.

### Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA. Skip link → `<main id="main">`; semantic landmarks; `aria-current` on
active nav; hamburger `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`; focus-trap + Esc + focus-return
in drawer; table `<caption>`/`scope`; callout kind by icon+label (not colour alone);
`:focus-visible` brand-blue ring; gold-deep for any small gold text.
